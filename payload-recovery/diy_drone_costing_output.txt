========================================================================================================
  COMPARISON - DIY vs used market vs contracting
========================================================================================================

  option                                 cash   build  RGB km2/d TH km2/night  legal envelope
  A: DIY RGB fixed wing                1,940E    40 h       64.2            -  Open A3, VLOS only
  B: DIY thermal quad                  4,390E    50 h          -          7.3  Open A3, VLOS only
  A + B (shared ground kit)            6,130E    90 h       64.2          7.3  Open A3, VLOS only
  Used M3T kit (prior analysis)        7,380E     0 h       37.7          5.3  Open A2/A3, VLOS
  Contracted C6 fixed wing           1,500E/d     0 h         45            -  STS-02 BVLOS, 2 km

  Notes the table cannot carry:
  - A DIY build has no C-class mark: STS-02 is unavailable AT ANY PRICE. The
    contracted operator's 12.6 km2/setup bubble cannot be replicated by building.
  - The M3T's RGB day rate (37.7) and DIY-A's (64.2) are both VLOS-limited in
    practice: ~0.8 km2 per setup, then relocate. Per-setup, all VLOS options tie.
  - Variant A's camera (24 MP APS-C at 2.9 cm) outresolves the M3T's RGB and
    every satellite option by an order of magnitude. Identification is trivial.
  - Build hours at engineer rates dwarf the cash gap: 90 h of integration is
    real money at any loaded rate. DIY wins on cash only if the hours are free.
  - One crash on day one: DIY-A loses ~1,470E and is repairable with foam and
    glue; the camera usually survives. An M3T crash is a total loss of 4,500E.

========================================================================================================
  VERDICT
========================================================================================================

  Total DIY cost, both aircraft, shared ground kit:  ~6,130 EUR + ~90 build-hours.

  The interesting split:

  - Variant A is the genuine bargain: 1,940 EUR for a machine that resolves the
    sphere at 24 px, flies 93-minute sorties, and repairs with hot glue.
    Nothing you can buy touches its cost-per-km2 - IF your hours are free.

  - Variant B is not really a drone project. It is a 2,950 EUR sensor purchase
    with 1,440 EUR of aircraft around it - and for 7,380 EUR the used M3T
    delivers the same 640-class detector already integrated, gimballed, weather-
    sealed and warrantied. DIY thermal only wins if you want the Boson anyway
    (radiometric raw access, future payload integration) - which, for a company
    that flies stratospheric payloads, is a genuinely defensible reason.

  Recommendation: build A, buy the M3T used, skip B unless the Boson has a
  second life planned on your balloon payloads - where a 100 g radiometric
  640 core is exactly the instrument a recovery beacon wants next to it.
